Committee headshots:

Usage:
- Replace files in `src/assets/` (e.g., `src/assets/ganesha-illustration.svg`, `src/assets/gallery/img1.svg`) with real images.
- Keep filenames or update `src/data/siteConfig.ts` to point to new paths.
- For production, optimize images (WebP/AVIF) and serve scaled variants.
